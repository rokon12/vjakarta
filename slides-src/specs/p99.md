# Scene `p99`: p99 latency, second by second

A strip chart of p99 latency for each second of a 30-second k6 run, drawn from the measured
series in `docs/series/`. It appears on three slides with three `mode` arguments. This is where
the talk's climax lands, so the `big.1` end frame must be the clearest frame of the deck.

Files you own: `scenes/p99.js`, `scenes/p99.css`, `slides/11-small.html`, `slides/13-big.html`,
`slides/22-rebuilt.html`, `tools/series_data.py`.

## The data

`tools/series_data.py` writes `const SERIES = {...}` between `// @data` and `// @end` in
`p99.js`. For each run: `p99` (per second, ms, of requests let in), `refused` (503s per second),
`failed` (other non-201 per second) and `summary` (the run's totals). `switched-big` also carries
`inFlight` (sampled once a second). Never edit the block by hand.

## The picture

- Time axis 0–30 s, labelled every 5 s. The sale is a `.plot-band` labelled `SALE`:
  10–16 s for the small sale, 10–13 s for the big one.
- Latency axis, logarithmic, 40 ms to 5 s, ticks at 50, 100, 500 ms, 1 s and 5 s. The 500 ms
  deadline is a `.plot-mark` labelled `LATE`.
- Each run is a `.plot-trace` through the middle of each second, named at its right end in
  `.plot-label`: blue for sixteen platform threads, red for virtual threads at the big sale,
  green for virtual threads in time (small sale, rebuilt). An earlier run shown for comparison
  is the same trace at 30% opacity.
- A pen (`.plot-pen-*`) rides the end of the trace while it plays.
- Readouts on the right (`div.readouts`, the contract's digit readouts), filled in when the
  trace finishes: `alarm` for the late and failed numbers, `good` for the ones in time.
- `rebuilt` only: under the time axis, grey `bar is-off` columns for the people turned away
  each second, labelled `TURNED AWAY`.

## Modes and steps

- `small`, steps 0–1. Step 0 plays `before-small` (blue), readout `p99 · 16 threads` 1018 ms.
  Step 1 plays `switched-small` (green) over it, readout `p99 · virtual` 72 ms.
- `big`, steps 0–1. Step 0 shows `switched-small` faint, readout `p99 · small sale` 72 ms.
  Step 1 plays `switched-big` (red) and freezes; readouts `p99 · big sale` 2325 ms, `Failed`
  939, `In flight` 829.
- `rebuilt`, steps 0–1. Step 0 shows `switched-big` faint, readout `p99 · big sale` 2325 ms.
  Step 1 plays `budgeted-big` (green) with the turned-away columns; readouts `p99 · rebuilt`
  114 ms, `Turned away` 991, `Failed` 0.

A play shows the 30 seconds of the run in 10 seconds of real time; the chart says `3× SPEED`.
With `animate` false, each step renders its finished chart and readouts at once.
